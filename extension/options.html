Tab Position

Move your active tab to a numbered position with a keyboard shortcut.

Tab behavior

Off: insert your tab and shift the tabs in between. On: exchange the two tabs, leaving the others in place.

Keyboard shortcuts

Assign positions 1–9 to Ctrl + Shift + the number. Use 0 for position 10. On Mac, use Command.

Shortcuts start unassigned. If a combination is reserved by Chrome or your operating system, choose another.

Positions count from the left, including pinned tabs. Moves across the pinned boundary do nothing. If the position exceeds the number of tabs, the last tab is the destination.

Settings stay on this device. No analytics or browsing data collection.
